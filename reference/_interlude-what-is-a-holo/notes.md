# What is a holo card? (interlude)

Video chapter: **8:35 - 11:11** of [All 39 Pokemon Card Holo Patterns Explained](https://youtu.be/wQ2TvnHVdys)
by **Sleeve No Card Behind**. No media extracted — this is the physics/production background
segment. Kept because it grounds the layer model the shaders imitate; what follows is a
reading of that segment, not a transcript of it.

Key facts for shader authors:
- Card = cardstock + nanometer-thin aluminum foil + ink. The PATTERN is rolled (embossed)
  into the foil as micro-textures that diffract light into color; tilting changes the
  wavelengths seen.
- Before patterning, the raw foil looks like the plain mirror holo.
- OPAQUE white ink blocks the foil (that is how the subject stays solid); colored inks are
  watered-down/translucent so the foil shines through them. Art direction = ink windows.
- Many visually different "reverse holo" designs share the SAME underlying foil pattern
  (e.g. vertical sheen) with different opaque-ink masks.
- The foil is supplied by external manufacturers; TPC designs the ink layers.
- Cards shown: Cleffa + Ancient Mew + a Magmortar LV.X (card-show anecdote - all three are
  Cosmos family), Shining Ho-Oh, reverse-holo examples (Sceptile, Magneton, ...).
