## Usage claim

Gold Secret Rare / Hyper Rare cards: SM-era gold item/energy secret rares ("full art
treatment with gold foil from top to bottom" — TCA Gaming holo history), SWSH-era gold
V/VMAX/item secret rares ("the pattern this time around makes the entire card gold —
background, text boxes, empty space" — same source), SV-era gold Hyper Rares, Mega-era
gold Mega Hyper Rares. Sleeve No Card Behind's gold-cards article: "special embossed
gold holofoil cards", secret-rare category, present in essentially every modern set.

## Notes

Full-face gold metallic foil with dense fine glitter grain (SWSH gold shows embossed
radial burst rays behind the art). The card body stays GOLD at every angle — the hue
travel is warm (pale yellow → saturated amber), not full-spectrum; the art elements
(here the rainbow energy wheel) carry the only non-gold color. A broad specular bloom
sweeps with tilt and the glitter grain twinkles inside it.
