# VSTAR pearl (vocabulary extension — not from the 39-pattern video)

Source: **[Pulling Arceus Vstar 123/172 from Brilliant Stars Pokemon TCG](https://youtu.be/wRDwJyv-aP8)**
by **Ant's Collectables** (YouTube, Mar 2022). Frames: 8 keyframes spanning the tilt demo
at 34.5s-42.5s; clip: 36.5s-40s (360p, silent).

## Cards / material shown

- Arceus VSTAR — Brilliant Stars 123/172 (regular VSTAR print) — hand tilt demo in front
  of the set's poster.

## Usage claim

Regular-print Pokémon VSTAR cards, Brilliant Stars through Crown Zenith (swsh9, swsh10,
swsh11, swsh12, swsh12.5). Bulbapedia (Pokémon VSTAR (TCG)): "Pokémon VSTAR cards have a
white, pearlescent border with gold accents … All Pokémon VSTAR cards are etched,
including the Regular prints. All Regular prints of Pokémon VSTAR depict the Pokémon
surrounded by a golden aura." Rainbow-rare and gold VSTAR prints are NOT this pattern
(they are rainbow-glitter / gold-secret respectively).

## Notes

White pearlescent full-face foil, etched texture. Under tilt, broad warm iridescent
washes (gold / pink / red) sweep the pearl body — the golden aura around the subject
glows hardest; framing borders flash narrow rainbow streaks. The VSTAR Power box stays
a dark anchor with its central star. Reads as "white opal", clearly distinct from the
black-bordered etched V/VMAX full-arts.
