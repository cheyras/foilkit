# Detective Pikachu (vocabulary extension — not from the 39-pattern video)

Source: **[Charizard 5/18 - Pokemon Detective Pikachu](https://youtu.be/WjuDazguHnE)** by
**Pokemon Holo** (YouTube, Feb 2021 — single-card tilt showcase channel). Frames: 8
keyframes spanning the tilt demo at 3.5s-11.5s; clip: 5s-8.5s (360p, silent).

## Cards / material shown

- Charizard — Detective Pikachu 5/18 (holo; movie-still artwork) — gloved-hand tilt demo.

## Usage claim

Detective Pikachu (det1, 2019): 18-card English movie set; every card is holofoil (every
pack contained 4 holos — Bulbapedia). The holo lives in the ART WINDOW of the standard
SM-era yellow-border frame. The Japanese Movie Special Pack equivalent (smp2) printed
its commons/uncommons as Mirror Holofoil instead — different treatment, out of scope.

## Notes

The artwork is a PHOTOGRAPHIC movie still printed translucently over a smooth
high-gloss foil. No discrete pattern elements: under tilt, broad soft iridescent beams
(diagonal in these frames) sweep across the art, and the photo's smoke/fire volumes
light up and dim as the sheen passes through them — the foil character follows the
photographic texture, giving a smoky/liquid look unique to this set. Closest physical
relative is the sheen family sheet behind photo ink rather than illustration ink.
Honesty note: the assignment-lane residual described this foil as "thick
shattered/raised"; no source for "shattered" was found, and the footage shows smooth
beams, not facets. Collectors do describe the det1 foil as notably thick/heavy stock;
the raised-feel claim stays unverified.
