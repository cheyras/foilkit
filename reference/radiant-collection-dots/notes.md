## Usage claim (per the video)

Radiant Collection (Legendary Treasures / Generations). NOT a unique foil pattern: dot overprint on top of ink + white-ink shape windows (Pikachu heads/hearts/bolts) + plain mirror foil beneath. Some RC cards are non-holo but still carry the dot overprint.
