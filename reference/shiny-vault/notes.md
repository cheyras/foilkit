# Shiny vault (vocabulary extension — not from the 39-pattern video)

Source: **[The Entire History of Shiny Pokémon Cards](https://youtu.be/_cyddOc1SMU)** (Jul 2026) by
**Sleeve No Card Behind** — the same creator as the 39-pattern corpus video.
Video chapter: **18:19 - 19:30** (the Hidden Fates shiny vault segment).
Frames: 8 keyframes spanning the tilt demo at 19:18-19:26; clip: 19:20-19:23.5 (360p,
silent). The frames are the video's split-screen: baby shiny (left) and shiny full-art
GX (right) tilted side by side.

## Cards / material shown

- Shiny Buzzwole — Hidden Fates SV24/SV94 (baby shiny, art-window scope) - tilt demo
- Shiny Ho-Oh GX — Hidden Fates SV50/SV94 (shiny full-art GX, full-face scope) - tilt demo

## Usage claim (per the video)

The shiny vault is the subset within Hidden Fates "packed full of alternate art shiny
cards"; baby shinies keep the classic illustration window, shiny GX are full-art. "Both
card designs are textured and feature the sparkles around the Pokémon." Continued in
Shining Fates (SWSH) with brighter yellow sparkles + a reddish-purple streak around the
subject (Bleeding Cool, "baby Shiny" article), and in Paldean Fates (SV) with holo
borders; Shining Legends (sm3.5) shinies are the SM-era precursor treatment.

## Notes

Silvery-white TEXTURED foil field (embossed even across the border on baby shinies)
scattered with printed shiny-sparkle glyphs — the in-game shiny animation's 4-point
stars and diamond outlines — around the subject. Under tilt the silver field flashes
soft pastel iridescence (cyan/blue edge tints on the Ho-Oh frames) and the sparkle
glyphs pop bright against it; no geometric foil pattern, no discrete color bands. The
glitter twinkle is finer and paler than rainbow-glitter's.
