- Alakazam ex (SV 151) - tilt demo

## Usage claim (per the video)

SV-era ex cards: dense star pattern printed ON TOP of the face; the foil layer underneath is just diagonal sheen.

## Notes

Different from starlight/starlight-ii: similar stars but many more, 'almost triple printed'. Collectors were disappointed.
